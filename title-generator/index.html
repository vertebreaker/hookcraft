The Science of YouTube Click-Through Rate (CTR)

Your thumbnail gets the initial glance, but your title confirms the decision to click. According to analytics across top creators, titles that perform best maintain a character count between 45 and 60 characters. Anything longer gets cut off with an ellipsis (...) on mobile YouTube feeds.

Three Title Mistakes That Kill Impressions

  • Repeating the Thumbnail Text: Your title and thumbnail should complement each other, not repeat the exact same sentence.
  • Bland Descriptive Titles: "My Workout Routine" generates no curiosity. "The 3 Exercises That Fixed My Shoulder (Permanently)" offers tangible proof.
  • Over-Promising Without Delivery: Pure clickbait spikes initial clicks but collapses retention within 10 seconds, causing the algorithm to stop serving impressions.
